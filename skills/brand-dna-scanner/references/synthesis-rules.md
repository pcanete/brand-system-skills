# Brand DNA Synthesis Rules

## Objective

Turn evidence into a reusable identity model.

## Avoid disconnected inventory

Weak synthesis:

Font: X
Color: Y
Photography: dark
Voice: premium

Strong synthesis:

The brand presents technical precision as cultural desirability.

This principle manifests through:

- concise authoritative language
- controlled editorial grids
- sculptural product photography
- restrained high-contrast color
- deliberate motion with little playful overshoot

## Core synthesis dimensions

Generate:

- strategic signature
- perceptual signature
- verbal signature
- visual signature
- photographic signature
- motion signature
- digital/interaction signature
- content signature
- product/physical signature when applicable

## Rule sets

Produce:

### Must preserve

Identity-defining.

### May adapt

Flexible.

### Must not introduce

Clearly incompatible.

### Channel-specific

Do not universalize.

### Campaign-specific

Temporary.

## Recognition model

Rank strongest distinctive assets.

## Confidence

The final synthesis must clearly state coverage and weak-evidence areas.
